- Contamination is flagged from free-text notes (agent_note/notes/protocol_notes) by keyword, so it is a screen, not a verdict. Read the flagged cells' notes. Most admissions so far are about seeing family SQL templates in bench/question_families.py while locating the dev-set rule.
