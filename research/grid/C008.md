## Method

A planner subagent wrote a metric spec (metric, population, filters, grain, time window, ties, rounding) and stated the revenue-cycle interpretation. A separate SQL-writer subagent saw only that plan and emitted three structurally different DuckDB queries. All three were executed. Every question agreed 3/3, so the first candidate was kept. There was no critic pass and no repair.

Few-shot examples were the three BM25-nearest solved questions outside the dev set, excluding the same parametric family and the held-out 27. Explore probes covered distinct payer classes and names, date ranges, frequency codes, null rates, and join cardinalities before the spec was written.
