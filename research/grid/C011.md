# C011

Config: `generation=direct_sql`, `fewshot=0`, `explore=0`, `candidates3=1`, `verifier=0`, `repair=0`, `assumptions=0`, `multiagent=0`. Model: Grok 4.7.

Dataset: default `bench.dataset.snapshot()` (generator 3.3, id `e21410ce6805`), read with a non-escalated `open_snapshot`. Dev set: 20 questions, sha256 order, excluding `heldout_hard_1` and `grokbot-r1`.

**Accuracy: 20/20** by `bench.compare.match` (deterministic checker, no LLM judge). Mean SQL executions: 3. Repairs: 0.

## How answers were chosen

Three SQL candidates were written, then executed. The kept query is the lowest-index candidate in the largest group of agreeing results. No candidate was rewritten after execution.

## Failure modes

No kept answer failed the matcher.

Candidate disagreements (the losing reading was not submitted):

- CO denial-reason totals: dropping the reviewed-denial CARC flag (`is_denial_carc`) added a 26th code. The agreeing pair kept the flag.
- Days from service to first submission: using the latest submission's date of service and payer class disagreed with using the earliest submission. The earliest-submission reading was kept.
- Days to pay: measuring the first submission to that payer regardless of responsibility disagreed with the first primary (`payer_responsibility = 'P'`) submission. The primary-only reading was kept.
- First-pass resolution: treating "paid > 0" as resolved disagreed with "exactly one event, current, and not denied". The not-denied reading was kept.

On the other 16 questions all three candidates agreed.

Gold SQL for several parametric families was visible in `bench/question_families.py` while the dev-set rule was being located (payer denial rate, payer-class denial rate, CPT denied lines, CPT share of line payments, payer paid in a month). Those items are not a clean direct-SQL trial. The six authored questions in this cell, plus the top-CARC and patient-responsibility questions, were not taken from an answer key.
