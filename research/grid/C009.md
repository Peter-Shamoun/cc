## Method

For each question: list the population, date basis, units, and tie rule and keep the standard revenue-cycle reading; probe payer names, grains, nulls, and join cardinality; write the answer as CTEs and check row counts, key uniqueness, and sums; execute three equivalent formulations and keep the result they agree on; then a critic pass checks population, units, and invariants and revises once (extra numeric columns removed; CO-45 kept out of denial-CARC lists). `repair=0`, so an execution error would have been a fail and was not retried. No candidate failed to execute.

## Assumptions that the answers use

- Denial rate is denied current adjudications over current adjudications (one claim per payer), a ratio, not a percent, unless the question asks for a percent.
- Money is current-adjudication CLP dollars, rounded to cents. Payer filters are exact `payer_name` or `payer_class` values.
- Service-line questions use `sem.remit_lines` on current adjudications. `line_is_denied` is the denied-line flag. Submitted procedure code is `procedure_code`.
- Reviewed denial reasons are `is_denial_carc` under group CO. CO-45 is a contractual write-off, not a denial reason.
- Latest-submission questions include voids. First submission is the earliest 837 date in the data. Primary payer is `payer_responsibility = 'P'`.
- First-pass resolution is exactly one 835 event, and that event is the current non-denied adjudication. The rate is a percentage rounded to 2 decimals.
- Days-to-pay percentiles are continuous (`percentile_cont`) and rounded to 1 decimal.

## Cross-checks that agreed

- Three denial-rate formulations matched, and `count(*)` equalled `count(DISTINCT claim_payer_key)` on current adjudications.
- UnitedHealthcare July plus August adjudications equal that payer's current row count (136 + 61 = 197).
- Cigna August claim paid equalled the sum of current service-line paid ($4,243.89). Aetna CLP patient responsibility equalled PR-group CAS ($2,570.75).
- Primary-payer lag rows sum to 2,336 claims with a primary submission. Paid days-to-pay rows sum to 1,101, matching primary current adjudications with paid > 0.
- First-pass pairs sum to 2,393, the number of claim+payer remits.
- The top five CARCs are the head of the full denial-CARC list. Adding every CO code only inserts CO-45.
- The two CPT-diagnosis formulations returned the same 327 rows. Eight procedures had a tied top diagnosis; the lowest code was kept. Claim-procedure pairs sum to 12,214.
