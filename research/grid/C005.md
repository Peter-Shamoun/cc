Each question was explored (values, nulls, grain), assembled as checked CTEs, then revised once by a critic pass on population, units, and invariants. The revised statement was executed once. `repair=0`, so that result stands.

## Assumptions (standard revenue-cycle reading)

- Current adjudication is `is_current_adjudication`. Denied is `is_denied` (recorded status 4 or signal v3).
- Primary payer is the 837 payer with `payer_responsibility = 'P'`, not `payer_sequence`. Claims with no primary submission are left out of primary-payer groupings.
- A claim is counted once at its latest 837 submission unless the question asks for every submission (replacements) or for the latest primary submission.
- Voided claims stay in submission populations. This snapshot has no frequency-8 void, so the void count is 0.
- Rates asked as a fraction stay in 0–1. Rates the question calls a percent are `100 * num / den`, rounded as specified.
- Deductible dollars are the sum of current PR / CARC 1 adjustment amounts on the primary payer, not claim-level patient responsibility and not `payer_sequence = 'primary'`.
- CPT payment share and denied-line counts use current 835 service lines (`sem.remit_lines`), which include adjudications that have no 837.
